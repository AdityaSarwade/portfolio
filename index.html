About & Journey

Who I Am

I am an Associate AI Engineer at Iron Mountain specializing in Generative AI, Autonomous AI Agents, and Scalable ML Engineering. As a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), I partner directly with enterprise stakeholders and engineering teams to design, evaluate, and deploy high-impact AI systems for clients including McLaren F1, Wells Fargo, the US Government, and the Saudi Arabian Government.

My work combines deep expertise in modern LLM architectures with high-performance systems programming in Python, Rust, and GoLang. I lead initiatives spanning hybrid AI search engines (embeddings, SQL, Knowledge Graphs, and GraphRAG), autonomous multi-agent pipelines with persistent memory using AutoGen and LangChain, and fine-tuning 7B–30B parameter models using quantization-aware techniques.

By architecting cross-language microservices in Rust and optimizing CPU inference via ONNX and DASK load balancing, I have delivered ~55% resource consumption reductions while supporting concurrency upwards of 3,000 users. I hold a B.Tech in Computer Science from Dayananda Sagar University, Bangalore.
